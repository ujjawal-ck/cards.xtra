  Short feedback goes in a Toast. Feedback that must stay with the flow goes in an InlineMessage. Both take their colour from a tone.
</PageHeader>

## The rule

- A `Toast` is one line, with an optional single action and a dismiss. It has no title (`src/molecules/Toast.tsx`).
- Today each screen mounts its own Toast (`src/screens/ReviewUsScreen.tsx:306`). There is no app-level host. A native `Alert` carries the errors in click-out, withdraw, gate and Help.
- An `InlineMessage` stays on the page, can have a title and up to two actions. Use it for a state the user must read before moving on (success after a change, a warning above a form).
- Tones: brand, gray, success, warning, error, info, purple, sky, rose (`colors.tones`). Use success, warning, error and info for feedback.
- Confirmation after a task ends in a sheet where the app has one: `FeedbackAppreciatedSheet` in the missing-cashback help flow (`src/screens/MissingCashbackHelp.tsx:169`).
- Haptics fire on press and result (`src/lib/haptics.ts`). They back the visible feedback and never replace it.
- Do not claim success the app cannot confirm. Edit Profile says "saved" but saves nothing (R5). Review Us shows "submitted" and discards the text (hidden, D14).
