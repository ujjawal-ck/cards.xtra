  A global strip says the device is offline. That is not enough: a screen with no cache must also say what is missing. Today most screens show a false empty or a false ₹0 instead.
</PageHeader>

## The rule

- `OfflineBanner` shows a strip at the top while NetInfo reports offline. Loaded data stays on screen (`src/molecules/OfflineBanner.tsx:16`).
- With no cache, TanStack Query pauses the query: it is pending, not loading and not error. Screens fall through to their empty branch. **Inferred from code, not reproduced on a device** (audit, systemic finding).
- Only `useCardSavings` handles this (`src/query/useCardSavings.ts:124`). The other screens in the audit table (twelve rows with Home) show "0 cards", "No offers here yet", "No orders yet", or ₹0.
- The tools already show the right state: "You're offline", "Check your connection and try again.", "Try again" (`src/screens/tools/ToolResultsScreen.tsx:536`). The proposal reuses it everywhere.
- Never render ₹0 for "unknown". Earnings offline shows the offline state in place of the masthead.
- Engineering first: one shared helper that turns "paused with no data" into an `isOffline` flag. Not a design-system part.

## Where it appears

- App shell: the strip on every screen (`App.js:204`).
- Flow 2 Home and discovery: Home, Offers, Search. Flow 3a: Cards you can get, All card benefits.
- Flow 4 and 5: tools (done), Advisor, Advisor results. Flow 7: Earnings. Flow 8: Orders, Order detail, Claims, Missing cashback, Choose date. Flow 9: Profile.
- Flow 10 Offline and session: the strip, paused screens, mid-flow return (not built).
