## The rule

- A screen that fails to load uses `ScreenState`: title "Couldn't load …", a cause line, and "Try again".
- While the retry runs, the action label reads "Retrying…" (Orders, Claims, Transactions).
- Name the cause when the user can fix it ("Check your connection") and own it when we caused it ("Something went wrong on our side").
- A field error is inline: `Input` with `state="error"` and a `hint`. Do not use a dialog.
- Native `Alert` for an error is the old way (click-out, withdraw, gate, Help). The audit proposes `InlineMessage` or `Toast` instead.
- A money error never leaves the outcome unclear. See Money confirmation.
- Gap: the Figma "Connection lost" and "Something didn't go as planned" illustrations are not exported, so these states have no picture.

## Where it appears

- Flow 1 Launch and login: send OTP failed, wrong OTP.
- Flow 2 Home and discovery: catalogue error, Offers error, Search.
- Flow 3a Card detail and apply: detail fetch failed, checker API failed, click-out blocked.
- Flow 7 Earnings and withdraw: Earnings error, balance, send OTP failed, payout failed.
- Flow 8 Orders and missing cashback: Orders, Claims, store visit lookup, invoice upload.
