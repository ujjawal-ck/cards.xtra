## The rule

- Account-only tabs (Earnings, Profile) show `GuestPrompt` to a guest: a title, a body about what they get, and "Log in".
- A guest who taps Apply or Shop & Earn sees `EarnLoginSheet`: "Log in to earn cashback" with a second choice, "Apply without cashback" or "Shop without cashback" (D13).
- The second choice always exists. Guests are not blocked from the revenue path, they only lose the cashback.
- Get Help asks a guest to log in before Missing Cashback (`src/screens/GetHelpScreen.tsx:121`).
- The login sheet itself is a flow, not a pattern part. See Flow 1.

- Flow 1 Launch and login: onboarding "Get started" as guest, "Already on CashKaro? Log in".
- Flow 3a Card detail and apply: Click-out, guest earn prompt.
- Flow 6 Offers: Store page Shop & Earn. Flow 7: Earnings guest. Flow 9: Profile guest, Get Help.
