  When the refresh token is rejected, the login sheet opens over the current screen. It does not say why, and the way back to a money step is not defined.
</PageHeader>

## The rule

- The sheet opens by itself with `source: session_expired` (`src/auth/LoginSheetProvider.tsx:125`). A manual logout never sets it.
- Today the title is always "Log in or sign up" (`:427`). A user whose session ended sees the same words as a new user.
- Proposal: a title and body for this source: "Please log in again", "You were logged out to keep your account safe. Your cashback is safe." This needs a title and body slot per source (DS gap).
- On expiry the cache is cleared, so an account-only screen left open shows its empty state behind the sheet (audit).
- Not designed: return to the money step (withdraw OTP, claim submit, invoice upload) after login (R17). The audit gives no copy, so this page shows none.
- A network failure keeps the session (`src/auth/AuthContext.tsx:127`). Only a rejected token expires it.

## Where it appears

- Flow 1 Launch and login: Login sheet, session expired state.
- Flow 7 Earnings and withdraw: Verify OTP, "session expired" (R17).
- Flow 8 Orders and missing cashback: claim submit, invoice upload.
- Flow 10 Offline and session: session-expired sheet, mid-flow return (not built).
