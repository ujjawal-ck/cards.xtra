## The rule

- Three results, three sheets: `EligibleSheet`, `MaybeSheet`, `IneligibleSheet`. They share `EligibilityMasthead` and show the details the user typed with an Edit link.
- A rejection shows a reason, up to three alternatives with different jobs (closest, highest Rewards, lifetime free) and "See all N cards you can get" (D22).
- The reason comes only from the card's own income bar. Otherwise a generic line shows, so a wrong reason never reaches the user (D22).
- "Continue to Apply" stays on the maybe and ineligible sheets (D22).
- Closing the checker during Apply drops the Apply, with no bank page and no message (R2).
- Apply from tools, advisor, Cards you can get and store best cards skips the gate Home runs (R11). Open product question: same gate everywhere?
- Gap: with zero alternatives (R15) blocks 2 and 3 are left out and the only exits are Edit and Continue.

## Where it appears

- Flow 2 Home and discovery: Eligibility checker (empty, validation errors, checking, error).
- Flow 3a Card detail and apply: Apply gate, Result sheets (eligible, maybe, ineligible).
- Flow 3b Rejection: Rejection sheet, Cards you can get.
- Flow 5 Card advisor: eligibility bridge, form, "likely to qualify", error.
