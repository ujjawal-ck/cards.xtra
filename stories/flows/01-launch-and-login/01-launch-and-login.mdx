<PageHeader title="1 Launch and login" meta="Flow 1 · 4 steps · 26 states">
Get into the app fast, as a guest or with a CashKaro account. The intro plays, first launch sees Onboarding, and the login sheet (one app-wide phone and OTP sheet) opens from Onboarding and from every login gate. Auth: guest to login. Money-sensitive: verifying an OTP on production creates or signs in a real account.
</PageHeader>

## Flow

```
ENTRY: app start                 other gates: Earnings, Profile, Get Help,
  |                              "Log in to earn", session expiry
  v                                    |
Splash (Lottie, 7 s safety timer)      |
  |-- signed in or returning guest --> MainTabs (Home)
  |-- first launch
  v
Onboarding (3 slides)
  |-- "Get Started" ------------------> MainTabs (as guest)
  |-- "Already on CashKaro? Log in" --+
  v                                   |
Login sheet  <------------------------+   [AUTH]
  phone -> Get OTP -> OTP (30 s resend)
  |      |-- new number: also asks for a name   [MONEY: creates an account, D16]
  |-- verify OK, returning --------------> MainTabs
  |-- verify OK, signup from Onboarding -> Greeting -> MainTabs
  |-- verify OK, from another gate ------> back to that screen (onSuccess runs)

[hidden] NameInput: no route links to it since D16 (not storied)
```

States: 23 live, 3 proposed, 0 hidden, 0 demo. Each state is a story; a row links to it. Screen stories use the real app screen with no network (fixtures answer every call); a proposed state is composed from design-system parts and is not in the app.

## Risks

- **R9**: New users get a generated placeholder email. Profile shows it masked. Payout, claim and reset emails never reach them. Seen in: Login sheet, new number.
- **R17**: A session that expires during a money step opens the login sheet, clears the cache, and the return to the step is not defined. Seen in: Login sheet, session expired (proposed copy).
- **R20**: OTP rate limit (5 per number, 10 per IP in 10 minutes) shows the raw API message with no retry time. Seen in: Login sheet, rate limit.

## Open questions

- 8. New users get a generated email (D16): collect a real email, and at which step (signup, first withdrawal, first claim)? Owner: product owner.
- Login sheet: should the session-expired sheet say why it opened, and does "Your cashback is safe" hold while a withdrawal may be in flight? Owner: product owner.
- Offline OTP request and rate limit: fixed 10-minute copy or generic "try later"? Owner: product owner with the backend team.
- Greeting: cap the name at signup, or ellipsize on the greeting? Owner: product owner.

Not storied: Splash "session loading" is shown as a signed-in splash because the screen has one visual state; the resend-ready state needs a real 30 second wait.
