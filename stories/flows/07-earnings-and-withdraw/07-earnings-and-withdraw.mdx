## Flow

```
Entry: Earnings tab (login needed; guest sees "See every rupee you earn")
       Profile "Withdraw Cashback" row (same gate)
   |
   v
Earnings ---- tap Withdraw ---->  $ Withdraw gate (useWithdrawGate)
                                    |-- payout settling      -> sheet, stop
                                    |-- nothing to withdraw  -> sheet, stop
                                    |-- below the minimum    -> sheet, stop
                                    |-- balance did not load -> native Alert, stop
                                    v
                              $ Method select (WithdrawMethodSelect)
                                  UPI | Bank Transfer (coming soon) | Gift card
                                    |                                   |
                                    v                                   v
                         $ Details UPI                        $ Gift card chooser
                         (UPI id + terms)                      |            |
                                    |                    Details Amazon  Details Flipkart
                                    |                    (mobile x2)     (email x2)
                                    +------------+-----------------+--------+
                                                 | Get Paid: send OTP (Alert on failure)
                                                 | terms link -> Terms (one screen, 4 copies)
                                                 v
                                      $ Verify OTP (WithdrawVerifyOTP)
                                         wrong | rate limited | connection problem
                                         payment failed | unknown outcome | session expired
                                                 v
                                      $ Success (stack reset, no swipe back)
Exit: Home (close or Continue Shopping)
      Earnings, after an unknown outcome (user goes back by hand)
Hidden: Payment history (D4), no route links to it
```

`$` marks a money step. Stories reset the shared store, cache and fetch mock when they mount, so each step is its own page. Open them from the tables below.

Stories that type or tap run a small DOM interaction after mount (a play function). They drive the real screen: no timers are patched and no state is faked. The native `Alert.alert` calls do nothing on react-native-web, so the live stories for those states show the screen after the failure but not the dialog. The alert text is written to the browser console. A `proposed` twin shows each failure as inline UI.

## Risks

- R4: after a withdrawal there is no per-payout record. Success goes to Home and Payment history is hidden. Earnings shows a Paid total and a settling sheet only.
- R6: offline cold start shows Confirmed ₹0 and Pending ₹0 on Earnings. A user with money sees nothing to withdraw. Inferred from code, not seen on a device.
- R12: Bank Transfer is "Coming soon", so a user with no UPI can only take a gift card.
- R17: the session expires during Verify OTP. The login sheet opens and the cache clears, but nothing returns the user to the step. This risks a lost payout attempt.
- Also seen in code: a 429 on the OTP check reads as "Enter a valid OTP", and the unknown-outcome message tells the user to check Earnings while no payout record exists (R4).

## Open questions

- Native alerts: should the six alert states become inline messages and a full screen (the proposed twins), and who owns the copy?
- Payout record (R4): which fields, and where does it live (Success, Earnings, Payment history)? Does CashKaro return a reference at accept time?
- Session expiry in a money step (R17): return to Verify OTP with the draft kept, or restart from the method list? Never resubmit without a fresh OTP.
- Bank Transfer (R12): hide the row until it works, or keep "Coming soon"?
- Payment history (D4): bring it back when a payout history API exists, or drop the screens?
- Email for new users (D16): collect a real email before the first withdrawal, since payout mail goes to a placeholder.
