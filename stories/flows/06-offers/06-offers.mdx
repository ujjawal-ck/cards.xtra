## Flow

```
Offers tab (MainTabs/Offers)
   |  category strip, 2-column store grid
   |  welcome promo sheet (once per session, only when stores exist)
   |  tap a store
   v
Store page (OffersBrandAmazon, any slug)
   |  about, cashback rates, how it works, timelines, terms,
   |  Best cards to use here, similar stores, FAQ
   |  share (OS share sheet)
   |  tap "Earn Cashback on <Brand>"
   v
$ Click-out (useClickOut, kind store, source store_page)
   guest: "Log in to earn" prompt, then the click continues
   signed in: link opens in the browser
   exit: store site or app, outside the app. Best cards opens card detail (flow 3a)
```

`$` marks a money step: the click-out carries the attribution (user id, click id). Its states live in flow 3a, Click-out.

Stories reset the shared store, cache and fetch mock when they mount, so each step is its own page. Open them from the tables below.

## Risks

- R10: the Offers tab may be empty on production, so every visit shows "No offers here yet". A whole revenue tab looks broken and store click-outs are lost. Doc only, not re-checked since 2026-11-09.
- R6: offline cold start with no cache. The tab shows "No offers here yet" while only the top banner says offline. Inferred from code, not seen on a device.
- R3 and R8 (click-out): a blocked click-out is silent, and there is no "Redirecting" step. Both are drawn in flow 3a.
- R11: Apply from the store page best cards skips the eligibility gate that Home runs.

## Open questions

- Should the empty state stay "No offers here yet" with an Explore cards action, or should the tab hide until stores are mapped on production (R10)?
- What does the user see before and after the store click-out: a redirect step, terms to accept, a return state (pm-flows question 5)?
- Should Apply from the store best cards run the same eligibility gate as Home (pm-flows question 2)?
