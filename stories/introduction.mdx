## How this book is organised

| Group | What is inside |
|---|---|
| Foundations | Tokens: colour, type, spacing, radius, shadow, icons, imagery, motion |
| Components | Every atom, molecule and organism, grouped by family, each with a playground |
| Patterns | Cross-cutting rules: loading, empty, error, offline, guest gate, click-out, OTP |
| Flows | Every screen of every journey, one story for each state |
