## How the layers work

A **primitive** is a raw value: `primitives.saffron[500]`. A **semantic** token is a role that points at a primitive: `colors.action.primary.default`. A component asks for the role and never for the raw value. To re-theme, change the semantic mapping in one place.

## Primitives

Two things to know when you read the "semantic" lines. Several steps share a hex: saffron 50 and 100 are both `#fff4eb`, so both swatches list the same semantic names. The transparent ramps repeat the values in `alpha` (opacity.ts).
