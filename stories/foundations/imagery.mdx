A tile marked "Not in the images registry" is a file in `src/assets/images` that no `images.*` key points to: it is used by a direct `require()` or it is unused.

Only three illustrations exist for `ScreenState` today: `appOrders`, `tracking`, `confirmation`. Figma has "Connection lost" and "Something didn't go as planned" frames that are not exported yet.
