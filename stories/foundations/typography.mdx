## D7: no font-size caps

Decision D7 in `docs/decision.md`: `maxFontSizeMultiplier` and `adjustsFontSizeToFit` misbehave on React Native's new architecture on Android. Text follows the OS setting and the layout grows with it. Known gap: no step-down rule for very long display amounts (for example 8 digits in the Earnings masthead).
