## Primitive and semantic

Colour has two layers.

1. **Primitives** are the raw palette: Saffron, Cobalt, Slate, Fern, Amber, Ruby, Rose, Violet, Sky and two transparent ramps. Never use them in a component.
2. **Semantic tokens** give a primitive a role: `colors.action.primary.default`, `colors.text.secondary`, `colors.feedback.error.text`. Components use only these.

Size, spacing, type, shadow and motion have one layer each: `spacing`, `radius`, `typography`, `elevation`, `motion`.

## Saffron and Cobalt (D17)

Decision D17 in `docs/decision.md`: Saffron is the only solid button colour. Cobalt stays for links, outline and secondary buttons, selected chips, and the opt-in `tone="accent"` fill. Figma draws Cobalt in some places. The app follows D17.

<Canvas of={Tokens.Rules} />

## Type (D7)

Decision D7: no `maxFontSizeMultiplier` and no `adjustsFontSizeToFit`. Text follows the OS font size and the layout grows. Details are on the Typography page.

## Gaps in the token layer

- Some semantic colours hold a raw hex with no primitive (see Colors, "no primitive").
- `gradients.ts` and `elevation.brand` hold raw hex values.
- `saffron[50]` and `saffron[100]` share one hex, and `primitives.transparentBlack` repeats `alpha.black`.
- There is no step-down rule for very long display amounts (D7 bans auto-shrink).
