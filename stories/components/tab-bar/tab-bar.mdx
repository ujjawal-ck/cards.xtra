## Composition

- BottomNav sits in `TabNavigator`, which places it above the home indicator. The component does not position itself.
- Header takes a `tabs` prop and renders TabPrimary under the header row. Do not stack a second TabPrimary under it.
- TabPrimary scrolls sideways, so 2 to 10 tabs all fit without truncation. TabsSecondary does not scroll: segments share the width equally.
- Press feedback on BottomNav is a scale to 0.92 with a spring. No size change between states, so labels never jiggle.
- Accessibility: BottomNav is a `tablist` with `tab` roles and a selected state.
