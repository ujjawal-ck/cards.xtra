## Composition

- `ListRow` has twelve types in one component. Add a type there. Do not build a new row for a single screen.
- `AmountActionRow` is a card (surface.default, radius.lg, elevation.light.xs). No `ListRow` type has the 48 px icon slot with a trailing button, so it is its own molecule. The button is the `Button` atom.
- `OrderRow` is a flat row with a bottom border, not a card. It reuses `LogoContainer`, `StatusBadge` and `Tag`.
- `DiscBulletList` is used in Store terms and conditions and in the Amazon terms bottom sheet.
- Haptics fire on press for `ListRow` and `OrderRow`. They do nothing on web.
