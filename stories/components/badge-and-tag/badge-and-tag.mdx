The other nine parts follow the same idea: a pale fill, a 1 px border or none, one short label, and a token colour.

| Part | Fill | Text |
|---|---|---|
| `Badge` | `colors.background.emphasis` or `gradients.saffronFade` | `colors.cashback.default` |
| `StatusBadge` | `colors.status[status].bg`, border and dot from the same block | `colors.surface.dark` |
| `CashbackBadge` | `gradients.saffronFade` | `colors.cashback.default` |
| `CashbackTag` | `gradients.saffronFade`, border `colors.cashback.tagBorder` | `colors.cashback.default` |
| `CardTypeTag` | `colors.tones.{success, warning, gray}.bgSubtle` | tone text |
| `WalletTag` | `colors.background.lavender`, border `colors.border.subtle2` | `colors.tones.brand.text` |
| `RankBadge` | `colors.action.secondary.default` | `colors.text.link` |
| `NumberBadge` | dark `colors.surface.inverse`, light `colors.surface.default` | inverse or `colors.text.secondary` |
| `TestimonialSavingsTag` | none; side lines fade `colors.background.lavender` to `colors.action.accent.default` | `gradients.primary` (needs a masked view) |

## Composition

- A label never holds another component. `CashbackTag` holds up to two brand logos and an info glyph (md, lg).
- `Tag` with a `status` prop sets its own tone, dot and shape. Do not also pass tone, size or icon.
- `StatusBadge` is for the cashback lifecycle only. For any other status colour use `Tag`.
- `CardTypeTag` on a card adds the word "card" and squares the right corners; place it flush to the card edge.
- Pass amounts already formatted (`formatINR`), with Indian grouping, to `WalletTag`, `CashbackBadge` and `CashbackTag`.
- Write a cashback rate as "X%" until someone verifies it.
- `Badge`, `CashbackBadge` and `CashbackTag` share one look (saffron fade) on purpose: do not use all three in one tile.
- Web: `TestimonialSavingsTag` depends on `GradientText` (masked view). It may not render the label on web.
