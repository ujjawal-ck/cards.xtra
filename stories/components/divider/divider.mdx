## Which one

| You are separating | Use |
|---|---|
| Rows in a list or blocks in a card | `Divider style="solid"` |
| A looser, decorative break | `Divider style="dashed"` or `"fade"` |
| Two alternative paths ("Or") | `Divider style="or"` (horizontal) or `DividerVertical style="or"` |
| A list from its label and count ("Your cards 3") | `Divider style="text-count"` |
| Two major floors of a page | `Divider style="section"` (8 px band) |
| Two blocks side by side (two stats) | `DividerVertical` |

## Composition

- A horizontal `Divider` fills the width of its parent. Give the parent a width.
- `DividerVertical` needs a parent with a row direction and a height; its own `height` prop sets the line length.
- `color` works on `solid` and `dashed` only (and the section band).
- Dividers carry no state. They are decoration; they have no accessibility role.
