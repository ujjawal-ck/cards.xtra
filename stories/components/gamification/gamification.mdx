No gamification component exists yet. This family is reserved for parts that reward repeat use, such as streaks, levels, progress toward a reward and unlocked badges. There are no stories on this page.
</PageHeader>

## What is reserved

- Parts whose job is to reward the user for coming back or for finishing a goal.
- Parts that show progress toward a reward the user can see and name.
- The family appears in the sidebar so a new part has a place from day one.

## Nearest parts today

None of these is a gamification part. They show a rank or a count, and a gamification part may build on them.

- `RankBadge` and `NumberBadge` in the Badge and Tag family show a rank or a count.
- `StepProgress` and `StepIndicator` in the Steppers family show progress through a flow.
- `MilestoneStat` in the Carousels family shows milestone images with the numbers baked into the artwork.

## Open questions for product

- Which reward loop comes first, and what is the reward in rupees or in access?
- Where does it show: Home, Earnings or Profile?
- Which measure counts toward it, and can it be gamed? Check the metric before the design.

When a part is built, add its stories under `stories/components/gamification/` and replace this page with a pick table and an anatomy.
