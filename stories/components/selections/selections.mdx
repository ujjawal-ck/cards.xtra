## Composition

- `Choice` = `Checkbox` or `Radio` + label + optional description. The row is the tap target. The inner control is visual only.
- `FilterRow` = two `Chip`s (Category, Filters with a dot) + an Eligibility switch. The switch is the React Native Switch with a saffron track, not the `Switch` atom.
- `InlineFilterQuestion` = title + `Chip`s (size 32) on a flat `surface.sunken` panel. A selected chip shows the card count through the `count` prop.
- `TermsAndConditions` = optional `Checkbox` + body12 text with blue link spans. `checked="none"` drops the box for passive copy.
- `Checkbox` and `Radio` have no group logic. The parent owns the value.
- `Selector` has a fixed 12 right padding so a long title wraps instead of splitting a word.
- `DateSelection`, `MonthSelector` and `Rating` come from Figma and no screen uses them today. `Rating` only shows a score.

## Open product questions

- `FilterRow`: should Eligibility move to the `Switch` atom, for one switch look across the app?
- `Rating`: is a tap-to-rate version needed? Today it is read-only.
- `DateSelection` and `MonthSelector`: keep, or remove with the next clean-up? No screen uses them.
