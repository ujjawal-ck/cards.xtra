## Composition

- The switch is controlled: pass `value` and `onValueChange`. The parent stores the value.
- Put it at the right end of a row with a label and an optional line of help text on the left.
- A switch with no visible label needs `accessibilityLabel`. A labelled parent row can supply it.
- Pressed fades to 0.85 opacity. A selection haptic fires on change.
- The setting applies when the switch flips. Do not add a Save button for it.
