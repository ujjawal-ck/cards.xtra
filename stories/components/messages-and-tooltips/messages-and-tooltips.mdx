## Composition

- `InlineMessage`, `StripAlert` and `Toast` share one tone system. Every colour comes from `colors.tones[tone]`. Do not pick a hex in a screen.
- `StripAlert` is full width and 32 px high. Place it flush against the block it belongs to.
- `Toast` has no timer and no position. The screen shows it with local state and hides it itself (about 3 s on payment history details).
- `Tooltip` draws only the bubble and the arrow. The screen decides when it shows, where it sits and when it hides (4 s on the Earnings masthead). Use `arrowAlign="end"` under a right-aligned info icon.
- `OfflineBanner` is mounted once in `App.js` next to the navigator. Do not mount it in a screen. It uses `colors.feedback.warning`, not a tone.
- `TrackMoreBanner` carries its own 16 px side inset. Do not wrap it in a padded container.
