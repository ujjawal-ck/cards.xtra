## Composition

- Every store block is 360 wide with `spacing[16]` side padding. The screen stacks the blocks and adds the dividers.
- `StoreOfferDetails` and `StoreBenefitDetails` both end with one shared `HowToGetOfferCard`. Do not copy the card into a block.
- `StoreCashbackTimelines` and `StoreSimilarStore` reuse the `Grid` organism (`cashback-timelines` and `brand-1x3` variants). `StoreTermsConditions` reuses the `HowItWorks` carousel and `DiscBulletList`. `StoreFaq` reuses `Accordion` and `Divider`.
- `StoreBestCards` mounts `CardDetailSheet` itself and opens it on a card tap, as Home does. It needs the card catalog query, navigation, and the sheet provider.
- `CardDetailSheet` is a platform dispatcher. iOS gets the two-stage sheet. Android gets `CardDetailScreenAndroid` (decision D18, card detail stays full screen on Android). Both keep the same ref contract: `present()` and `dismiss()`.
- Several blocks hide themselves when they have no data: `StoreFaq` and `StoreTermsConditions` with an empty list, `StoreBestCards` while the catalog loads.
- `NeverMissCashbackNudge` and `CashbackPip` render only on Android. In Storybook on web they are blank.
