## Which one

| You are building | Use |
|---|---|
| The sticky bar with the main action of a screen (Continue, Withdraw, Apply) | `FooterButtons` |
| A strip with a note or a terms checkbox above the actions | `FooterButtons` with `strip` |
| The closing headline, supporting text and card art at the end of a long page | `Footer` |

`Footer` uses `display40ExtraBold` for the headline and `heading20SemiBold` for the subtext, both at `opacity[32]`. The padding is `spacing[24]` on top and `spacing[16]` on the sides. The theme sets the text colour: `dark` gives `surface.dark`, `light` gives `text.inverse`.

## Composition

- `FooterButtons` is pure composition: every button is the `Button` atom, the strips are `StripAlert` and `TermsAndConditions`.
- `QuizStep` pins a `FooterButtons` (single) at the bottom of its screen.
- The solid button is saffron only. Use `outline` for the secondary action.
- `Footer` is theme-only, not tone-themed. Its text colour flips with the surface it sits on, so place `light` over a dark surface or an image.
- `Footer` takes children, usually card art, which render below the CTA.
