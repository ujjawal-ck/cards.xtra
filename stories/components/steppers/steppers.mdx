## Composition

- `StepIndicator` is built into `Stepper`. A step sets `indicator` to override the marker that its state would give.
- `StepProgress` is built into `QuizStep` (header row), and `QuizStep` pins a `FooterButtons` (single layout) at the bottom.
- `Timeline` and `ProcessSteps` draw their own tiles and connectors. They do not use `StepIndicator`.
- `Timeline` is for status with dates: 44 px tone circles and a dashed connector. `ProcessSteps` is an explainer: 44 px numbered saffron squares and a dotted connector. `Stepper` is progress: 20 px markers and a solid connector.
- `Stepper`, `ProcessSteps` and `Timeline` are vertical. The horizontal "How it works" scroller is the `HowItWorks` export of Carousels.
- `QuizStep` has no screen using it today. It lives in the gallery only.
