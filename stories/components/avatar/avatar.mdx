Swipey, the mascot of the card advisor: a friendly credit card with a face, arms and legs. It shows what the advisor is doing with six states. The screen passes the state. The avatar never decides it.
</PageHeader>

## States

| State | What the user sees |
|---|---|
| `idle` | Gentle float, a blink every few seconds, a rare small wave |
| `listening` | Slight tilt, attentive eyes, soft glow |
| `thinking` | Eyes up, moving brows, small sparkles |
| `speaking` | Mouth moves, gentle bounce. `audioLevel` sets how far it opens |
| `success` | Happy eyes, arms up, sparkles |
| `error` | Concerned face, one small shake |

## Composition

- The outer view is a square of `size` with `accessibilityRole="image"` and a label ("Swipey, your card advisor" plus the state in words).
- The parts are internals of `src/organisms/creditCardAdvisorAvatar`: `Body.tsx` (card body, arms, legs, glow, sparkles), `Face.tsx` (eyes, brows, mouth) and `useAvatarMotion.ts` (the shared values for each state). Do not import them from a screen.
- Pose maths and geometry are in `src/lib/advisorAvatar/pose.ts`. The state contract is `src/lib/advisorAvatar/types.ts`.
- Motion runs through reanimated, on iOS with reduce motion off. On Android and with reduce motion on, each state shows one still pose. On web the Storybook preview runs the motion.
- The advisor chat maps its signals to a state in `avatarStateFor` (`src/lib/advisor/transcript.ts`). Priority: error, thinking, success, speaking, listening, idle.
