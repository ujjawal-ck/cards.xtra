## Composition

- **Snap points.** The shell uses 58% and 96%. Info sheets (earnings, claims, logout, feedback) use dynamic sizing: they hug their content and do not expand. Other sheets fix their height: filters 70%, verdicts 90% (red 95%), eligibility checker 94%, edit spends 90%, savings breakdown 75% and 96%, card detail 75% and 100%.
- **Sticky footer.** Put the main action in `footer` with `footerProps`. The body gets bottom room (`spacing[96]`) so the last row is not hidden. Solid buttons are Saffron (decision D17).
- **Header first.** Use `BottomSheetHeader` for the handle. Do not draw a second handle.
- **Back handler.** No sheet in `src/organisms` registers its own `BackHandler`. Closing by back, backdrop or pan down is the library's. The RN Modal sheets (`HowItWorksSheet`, `ShareSheet`, `SearchTopSheet`, `PromoTopSheet`) close through their own Modal and their own `onClose` or `onDismiss`.
- **One sheet at a time.** Hosts close one sheet before they open another. Example: Share dismisses the card detail, then brings it back when Share closes (`HomeScreen.tsx`, near line 2570).
- **Modal provider.** Every gorhom sheet needs `BottomSheetModalProvider` above it. The Storybook preview adds it. Sheets that call `useNavigation`, `useQuery` or `useAuth` need the harness (`ComponentHarness`).
- **Present on a ref.** Modal sheets show nothing until `ref.present()`. Each story calls it after mount. `SavingsBreakdownSheet` and `EditSpendsSheet` take their data in `present(...)`.
